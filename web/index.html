Deals

A deal is listed in the newest scan at 70% or less of what it sells for: the lower of the market value that scan measured and the middle of the item's lowest prices on at least three earlier days. Profit is per unit, after the auction house's 5% cut. Goods merchants sell are no deal. Vendor deals need the vendor prices the game shows, which only the addon has: type /gb deals in game to see them.

Movers

Items in the newest scan whose lowest price differs from their lowest price on the day before they were last seen. Prices under a silver on both days are left out.

    Download Goldbook

    The Goldbook app installs the World of Warcraft addon, keeps it up to date, and uploads your scans and characters to this site. The game only lets an addon save to disk, so without the app the prices stay in the game.

    Only the addon

    To install the addon by hand, download it from GitHub and copy its Goldbook folder into Interface/AddOns of your game. Your prices then stay in the game until you install the app.
